## 0. Outcome

1. **(b) resolved NEGATIVELY.** Komaki (2015) Theorem A1, eq. (29), lists the
   prior-independent O(N^{-2}) terms explicitly: Efron (e-embedding) curvature,
   mixture embedding curvature (norm and squared mean), skewness norm, a
   fourth-moment contraction, and a mixed moment. These are generically nonzero
   for one-parameter models, where every Riemannian curvature invariant
   vanishes; hence the term is not proportional to R and Perelman's F is not the
   integrated predictive risk. The identity of record is restricted to the
   gradient term (v2 K1, K2).
2. **(a) v2 written** as a research note with six status-tagged claims:
   K1 Komaki–Perelman gradient identity (sign corrected); K2 the negative result;
   K3 shrinkage priors ⇔ non-parabolic Fisher manifold (Komaki); K4 Brown's
   admissibility integral = parabolicity test of the weighted manifold
   (R^d, flat, m dx), machine-checked (C23), m ≡ 1 giving Stein's d ≥ 3;
   K5 Komaki's conformal frame = v1's "Einstein frame", coefficient corrected;
   K6 parameter dimension. Four geometries named; horizon statements
   restated per geometry; the v1 formalisms tabulated as options with status;
   the absence of any dynamics explained structurally (Fisher metric fixed by
   the model; updating moves the measure, not g).

## 1. The impostor test as a contract/agreement structure (2026-08-16)

Owner's framing, raised after the AR-020e sector results: the stationary-
impostor test behaves like a **contract negotiation** — the question is not
a property of the state alone but of **what the parties admit as a
description**.

- The observer holds the emergent geometry (the MI metric — what is
  visible from outside). The system holds its dynamics (reachable sectors,
  conserved charges). The impostor question: *is there a stationary state
  satisfying both parties?*
- Class-resolved answer (AR-020d/e, in-model):
  - **Chaotic:** negotiation trivial — any thermal clause works (ETH as
    "standard boilerplate everyone signs").
  - **Integrable quench:** contract needs riders — the conserved charges
    written in explicitly (GGE), then it closes.
  - **Quasiperiodic:** *no honest contract.* The only stationary state
    matching the observer's clause (geometry) violates the system's clause
    (stay in the dynamically accessible magnetization sector) — the
    near-exact commutant impostor is a forged signature (weight in sectors
    the dynamics never populates; T3 collapse 0.003 → 0.22 at N = 10).
- The dephasing sign structure reads as **third-party enforcement**: weak
  energy-monitoring noise *pins* precisely the structure that has no
  admissible stationary counterpart, while degrading the chaotic
  structures that never needed enforcement.
- Conceptual neighbor to check properly if AR-022 runs: **Zurek's quantum
  Darwinism / einselection** — objectivity as what many observers can
  independently agree on, with the environment selecting what survives to
  be agreed upon. Our twist: agreement is always relative to an
  *admissible algebra* of descriptions; widening the algebra beyond what
  the dynamics licenses lets observers "agree" on fictions. (Zurek
  citations to be verified before any use — none checked yet.)
- General survey question this suggests: in philosophy-of-science terms,
  is "actively maintained vs merely instantiated" a fact about the object,
  or about the description-class two parties agree to quantify over?
  Relate to: structural realism (structure vs carrier), Dennett's "real
  patterns," functionalism's substrate-independence claims — all
  UNVERIFIED pointers, to be sourced.

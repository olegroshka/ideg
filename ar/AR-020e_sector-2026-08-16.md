- **Date:** 2026-08-16 (N = 10 complete; N = 12 running at packet time,
  results slotted on landing)
- **Trigger:** third adversarial audit (owner-run multi-model, internal)
  of the round-2 draft — three criticisms accepted after independent
  verification: (i) "block coherence" is basis-relative (within a
  degenerate block the eigenbasis is arbitrary, so any stationary σ is
  diagonal in some valid eigenbasis); (ii) the commutant impostor may
  rely on magnetization sectors the dynamics cannot reach (the

  spot checks (qp ≈ 0.465 both channels; integrable Haar 0.51 block
  vs ~0.9 full-diag) were reproduced before acceptance.
- **Instrument:** `scripts/ar020e_sector_suite.py`, stages `channels`
  and `sector`; outputs `results/AR-010/ar020e_*.json`.

## Stage: sector (qp + integrable × 20 runs × both sizes)

Nested admissibility tiers, identical optimizer machinery
(block-Cholesky, analytic gradients; per-sector normalization gradient
FD-verified at 1.4e-4):

- **T1** commutant of {H} (243 E-blocks → Σ|B|² = 7776 at N = 10)
- **T2** commutant of {H, N_mag} (648 (E,q)-blocks, Σ|B|² = 2424)
- **T3** T2 + sector weights pinned to the run's Tr(ρΠ_q)
  (for one-magnon states: diagonal on N nondegenerate magnon levels)

| class | T1 | T2 | T3 | T1 weight outside run sectors | T1 ‖[σ,N_mag]‖ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| integrable | 0.0077 | 0.0086 | 0.030 (20/20) | 0.000 median | 0.272 med |

Validation battery on every optimized state: unit trace (err ≤ 2e-15),
block positivity (min eig ≥ 0), sector weights, mean energy, objective
recomputed from the fully assembled ρ via the mixed-state metric path
(max deviation 2e-15 from the optimizer's internal value).

**Findings:** (1) T2 = T1 run-by-run — cross-sector *coherence* is
never used; (2) the commutant impostor places a median 69% of its
weight in magnetization sectors the dynamics never populates; (3)
pinned to the accessible sector, the qp match collapses to the
population-only plateau (~0.24), as it must (one-magnon stationarity
⇒ diagonality on N nondegenerate levels); (4) the integrable
impostor is sector-honest — the control's control.

**Corrected claim of record (seventh claim-level correction):** the
class discriminator is **admissibility** — which stationary algebra
the impostor may live in. Restricting the comparator to the conserved
magnetization sector destroys the near-exact commutant match and
returns it to the threshold-marginal population-only plateau: no
NEAR-EXACT sector-admissible impostor of the quasiperiodic metric was
found. (At the registered eps_Phi = 0.25 itself the T3 counts
straddle: 15/20 at N = 10, 9/20 at N = 12 — precision of statement
per the fourth-audit wording check, 2026-08-16.) "Block coherence as resource" is
retired (basis-relative); sufficiency language everywhere (all misses
are found upper bounds).

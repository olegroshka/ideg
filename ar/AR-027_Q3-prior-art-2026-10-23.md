criterion is the recurrence of the Dirichlet form ∫|∇j|² f* dx (Brown 1971; Johnstone 1986; Eaton
1992; Takanashi–McAlinn 2023); in Riemannian language this is parabolicity of the weighted manifold
(R^m, δ, f* dx), and Brown's §6 tests are the sphere-area parabolicity tests of Grigor'yan's survey —
a translation we have not found stated." K1, K5, K7 may be called "not found in the literature
searched", never "new", until a citation-index sweep (Google Scholar / MathSciNet "cited by" on Brown
1971 and Komaki 2006) is done — not possible from this environment.

## 2. What the adjacent literature already contains (for the note's related-work paragraph)

- **Brown 1971** — the object (energy, exterior Dirichlet problem, recurrence; §6 tests).
- **Srinivasan 1981; Johnstone 1984, 1986** — the Brown programme carried to exponential families and the Poisson case; Johnstone's title says "Dirichlet principles", i.e. the minimum-energy (capacity) formulation, for birth–death chains.
- **Eaton 1992 → Hobert–Robert 1999 → Eaton–Hobert–Jones–Lai 2008** — admissibility of formal Bayes rules ⟺ recurrence of a symmetric Markov chain on the parameter space; Dirichlet forms; Lyapunov-function recurrence criteria (the discrete-time cousin of Grigor'yan's exhaustion-function Thm 7.6).
- **Takanashi–McAlinn 2023** — Dirichlet-form recurrence ⟺ admissibility for symmetric Lévy location models under KL loss; the most explicit modern statement of "Brown = Dirichlet form".
- **Evans–Stark 1996** — Stein's inadmissibility for d ≥ 3 via stopped Brownian motion (a different bridge).
- **Sturm 1994** — Grigor'yan-type volume criteria for strongly local Dirichlet forms (covers W_F).
- **Choi–Mullhaupt 2014/2015** — superharmonic priors on Kähler manifolds (curvature enters only through the Kähler Laplacian).

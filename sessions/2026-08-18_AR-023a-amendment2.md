## Self-corrections recorded (SC-005)

1. **C1 was drift-blind.** My first repair proposal would have deleted
   the only diagnostic sensitive to calibration drift. Caught in
   adversarial review before implementation; the data then showed that
   term dominates the floor.
2. **C3 draft conflated bias with variance** — applied a 0.02 *bias*
   tolerance per experiment when per-experiment SD is 0.015. Caught by
   evaluating the proposal (73/100) rather than asserting it.
3. **A2.3 reused a threshold across a changed statistic.** Gated the
   *maximum* per-RDM correction against 0.05, a number calibrated for
   the *median* — the exact error the adversarial pass had named, then
   committed anyway. Margin was 1.9%; owner ruled the median (5×).
4. **Amendment 2 was not fully ported into S2 (floor).** The first S2
   condition ran under the retired matrix-norm floor (0.1338, 0/100).
   All results from that path discarded, not reinterpreted.
5. **Amendment 2 was not fully ported into S2 (leakage).** S2-G1 was
   judging the simulator's *exact* survival — a quantity that does not
   exist on hardware — instead of the corrected measured witness. Fixed
   at the aggregation layer so all conditions are judged uniformly.

6. **A2.3's retirement of the S2-G3 endpoint-shift half was not
   applied in aggregation**, so S2-G3 reported FAIL on a criterion the
   amendment had retired. Both adjudications recorded.

Root cause of 4–6: two independent implementations of the same
quantities. Detection worked (cross-checking a shared number across
paths), but detection is not a fix. **Resolved this session** by
unifying onto `scripts/analysis.py`; the guards now forbid a second
implementation rather than merely comparing two.

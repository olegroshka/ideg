## Outcome summary

1. **S1 CLOSED** at both shot settings, all four gates adjudicated:
   G1 **PASS** (0.00590 / 0.00641 < 0.02), G2 **FAIL** (0/100 both),
   G3 **FAIL** (0.11375 / 0.11013 ≥ 0.05), G4 **PASS** (byte-identical
   replays, `f503dfbe…` / `4fb7f89b…`).
2. **A1.1 escalation exhausted and quantified:** 896 shots moved the
   floor 3.2% for 16.7% more shots (1/√N). Passing G3 by shots alone
   needs ≈ 4,000 shots/circuit ≈ 1,850 QPU s — over 4× the 450 s cap.
3. **Root cause measured, not conjectured:** the frozen floor is a
   full-matrix Frobenius discrepancy; ε_sector is a scalar responding to
   one direction. Decomposition of the same noise: full norm 1.903 vs
   signal-direction projection 0.244 = **7.8×**; 7.8² ≈ 61 ≈ the
   effective independent directions of a 10×10 symmetric zero-diagonal
   metric. Observed floor ÷ endpoint SD = **8.0**. Both sides scale as
   1/√N, so no shot budget reconciles them.
4. **Second defect:** the projection criterion penalises a bias
   *correction* — projected estimator bias **+0.0059**, unprojected
   **+0.1004**. The ~0.09 "projection shift" is the projection removing
   a 0.10 bias.
5. **The physics never wavered.** Separation Δ resolved above floor in
   **100/100** ideal experiments (worst CI lower bound +0.0329) and
   **99/100** at the S2 operating point; zero LOTO/LOPO sign flips in
   200 experiments; leakage GREEN; raw vs M3 direction agreement
   100/100.
6. **S2 CLOSED** (11 conditions × R = 100): G1 **PASS**, G2 **FAIL**
   (0/100), G3 **FAIL** (proj. shift 0.0409 ≥ 0.02; per-RDM 0.00111
   passes), G4 **PASS** (100/100). Same signature as S1 — physics gates
   pass, rule-calibration gates fail. Envelope and the L4 path-quality
   pre-commitment (median 2q ≤ 6e-3, readout ≤ 2e-2, max edge ≤ 1e-2)
   in the findings packet §4; `s2_report.json` records
   `l4_requirement: null` because its automated derivation keys on the
   defective success rule.
7. **C6 — blocking design gap found:** AR-023 §5's leakage traffic
   light ("probability the measured bit string has exactly one
   excitation", from covering-array rows only) is **not computable** —
   no all-Z row exists in the 27-setting array (max 6 of 10 sites in
   Z), and the GF(3) construction cannot produce one. Red leakage is a
   registered kill criterion, so this blocks hardware. Costed fix: one
   added all-Z setting → 1,372 circuits, 370.8 s at 768 shots / 432.3 s
   at 896 — both inside the 450 s cap. This session's S2-G1 verdict
   used exact simulator survival (unavailable on hardware and
   readout-blind); on the hardware-computable subset witness it is
   AMBER at readout ≥ 2e-2.
8. **Amendment candidates C1–C6 PROPOSED, not applied** (they alter
   frozen decision rules or the measurement design — owner ruling).
   Counterfactual under C1 + corrected C3: **95/100 at 768**
   (boundary) and **99/100 at 896** → S1-G2 would pass, 896 the safer
   operating point. **S2 is not rescued at 768** (66–86/100,
   conservative proxy) — the binding sub-criterion there is the
   leave-one-pair-out excursion, which C5 shows is partly *structural*:
   on the exact noiseless metric, removing pair (0,9) already shifts ε
   by 0.0261, above the 0.0206 tolerance A1.3 implies at the S2
   operating point.

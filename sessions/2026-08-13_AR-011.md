## Findings

**A — VERDICT-PRESERVING, INTERPRETATION-INVERTING (the session's main
result).** The recorded numerators show the diagonal-ensemble comparator
responds LESS than the dynamical state in absolute perturbed drift under
every protocol at N = 10 (quench: comp 0.065–0.081 vs dyn 0.12–0.94;
dephasing: comp 0.20–0.42 vs dyn 0.19–1.08; loss: comp ≡ 0 vs dyn
0.14–1.19). The AR-010 packet's reading — "the stationary comparator is
far more fragile; the motion holds Φ against perturbations that scatter
the motionless twin" — is REFUTED: the direction came entirely from the
log-ratio denominator (the comparator's unperturbed drift is exactly 0,
floored at δ_floor = 1e-3, inflating its log ρ by ~ln(δΦ/10⁻³)).
Three sub-findings:
1. The §5.3 check-2 arm-1 verdict ("robustness differs, disjoint CIs")
   STANDS as preregistered — the criterion asks for a difference, not a
   direction, and the difference survives any instrument choice
   (numerator-only contrast: dyn > comp, also cleanly separated).
2. The floor-free switch-off arm carries the real sustained-by signal:
   dephasing at t_off moves Φ by 43–90% of ‖D̄‖ (chaotic 0.54,
   scrambling 0.52, localized 0.90, metastable 0.90, integrable 0.85,
   quasiperiodic 0.43) — killing the motion CHANGES the geometry, it
   does not merely freeze it.
3. **Spec-assumption refutation (§4.1):** the comparator is NOT
   "matched to the run's long-time-averaged Φ by construction" — MI is
   nonlinear in the (correctly time-averaged) RDMs, so Φ[ρ̄] sits
   43–90% away from D̄. The comparator never isolated "same geometry,
   no motion"; §4.1's matching language is wrong as written (the
   two-site RDMs match the time average; the MI graph does not).
   Consequence: sustained-by evidence should be stated as "the running
   geometry g* is not the frozen state's geometry, and the classes
   respond differently relative to their own baselines" — NOT as
   absolute fragility of the comparator.

**B — SURVIVES.** Metastable Ξ identical to machine precision under
degeneracy tolerances 1e-8/1e-10/1e-12 at both criterion sizes (N = 10:
[0.00373, 0.00592]; N = 12: [0.00475, 0.00617]) — the doublet splitting
sits far below every candidate tolerance at N ≥ 10; the N = 8
bimodality was size-specific and N = 8 is not a criterion size. The
(i, iv) separation and §5.3 check-1 margins are physical.

**C — Φ partition-dependence QUANTIFIED.** Nonlocal two-site frame
changes (three disjoint adjacent pairs) move Φ by 9.3% mean / 11.0% max
(chaotic, N = 10) and 17.4% / 19.7% (localized) — the same order as
ε_Φ = 0.25. Standing scope wall: Φ is a geometry of the posited
factorization, not of the state alone (TH-037 caveat now quantitative).

# arXiv quant-ph endorsement — candidate shortlist (working note)

Status: PARKED for owner action (2026-08-14). Strategy + ranked
candidates; contact emails are on the first page of each person's
recent arXiv PDFs. Send to one or two at a time, never a blast; link
the repo and compiled PDF (public), attach nothing. The endorsement
note template is `paper/ENDORSEMENT_NOTE.md` — tailor the opening
sentence per recipient (the personalization carries the response rate).

## Why this can work

Cold endorsement requests get ignored — except when the requester's
paper genuinely builds on the endorser's own work, the record is
public and serious, and the ask is explicitly small (endorsement is a
category-appropriateness assertion, not a review). Our citations are
load-bearing, the repo is public with the full preregistration trail,
and the PDF is journal-format.
